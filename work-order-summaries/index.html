Best AI for Field Service

Best AI for field service reports and work order summaries

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

Almost every field service suite now drafts the work order summary. The differences are how the technician gives the input, voice or typing, and where the result lands. If technicians dictate on site and the report writes itself into the system of record, the paperwork is gone. If they still have to open a form and press a button, it is only shorter.

  • Kenner: writes the report from a voice note and puts it, with parts and follow-ups, into your FSM, ERP or CRM. Our product.
  • Microsoft Copilot in Dynamics 365 Field Service: work order summaries, and updates by voice or text, included in the licence.
  • Salesforce Agentforce for Field Service: drafts the post-work summary and the pre-work brief in Salesforce.
  • Simpro Lightning: captures the job from the technician talking and sends the customer a summary.
  • IFS Cloud Field Service Management with IFS.ai: a work briefing before the job and an AI summary on the service report.
  • SAP Field Service and Asset Management with Joule: equipment, activity and service call summaries and a daily briefing.
  • Aquant: documents the repair automatically and updates the service record.

Briefs before, summaries after

There are two pieces of paperwork AI now handles. The brief before the job pulls together the customer, the asset and what happened on the last visits. The summary after the job turns what the technician did into a record the office and the customer can read.

Salesforce, IFS, SAP, Simpro and Kenner do both. Microsoft documents the summary and the record update.

What to check before you rely on it

Ask where the text ends up. A summary that sits in a chat window still has to be copied into the work order. Ask whether a person confirms it before it is saved: Microsoft, for example, has the technician confirm updates. And ask whether regulated records are covered. Kenner asks for what a refrigerant record needs after a leak repair, which a generic summary will not.

Questions and answers

Can AI write the service report from a voice note?

Yes. Kenner and Simpro Lightning both describe writing up the job from the technician speaking, and Microsoft Copilot updates the work order from speech. In each case it is worth having the technician read it back before it goes to the customer.

Is a free option available?

Microsoft includes Copilot in the Dynamics 365 Field Service licence, so there is no separate charge if you already pay for that. We found no stand-alone free tool for field service reports on the vendor pages we checked.
