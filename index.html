Best AI for field service in 2026

Every field service suite has AI features. Troubleshooting from manuals and guides is a commodity. The real gains come from capturing your own field service knowledge or getting AI to take action as well as give advice. This guide compares 12 tools based on published content.

The short answer

Pick on three things: does it take action or only advise, will your technicians actually use it, and does your field knowledge become a company asset. If your technicians already live in your suite's app, start with the suite's own AI. If not, look at an assistant they text or call for easy adoption: Kenner or Aquant.

  • Teams that want easy adoption and to capture their know-how: Kenner
  • Service organisations of equipment manufacturers: Aquant
  • Enterprises with 1,000 or more technicians and complex equipment: Neuron7
  • Teams on Salesforce Field Service: Salesforce Agentforce for Field Service
  • Teams on Dynamics 365 Field Service: Microsoft Copilot in Dynamics 365 Field Service
  • Teams on ServiceNow: ServiceNow Field Service Management
  • Teams on IFS Cloud: IFS Cloud Field Service Management with IFS.ai
  • Teams on SAP: SAP Field Service and Asset Management with Joule
  • Teams on Oracle Fusion Field Service: Oracle Fusion Field Service
  • Asset-centric service teams on ServiceMax: PTC ServiceMax AI
  • In-house maintenance teams: MaintainX Assist
  • Trade contractors on Simpro: Simpro Lightning

What we compared

  • What it does. Advice only, a drafted summary, or the task itself: the part ordered, the report written, the quote or invoice prepared. And whether that lands in one suite or in every system the job touches.
  • How a technician reaches it. Inside the field service mobile app, in a messenger, or on a phone call. A tool nobody opens on site helps nobody, and it records nothing either.
  • What it answers from. Only the records in one suite, or your manuals, past jobs and expert knowledge wherever they live. This decides whether it can help with the fault or only with the paperwork.
  • What you need first. Most suite AI needs that suite, and often an add-on licence on top. A specialist needs a connection to your systems, so ask how long that takes.
  • Price. As published by the vendor. Most do not publish one.

The full method, and what this guide does not claim, is on the method page.

1 Kenner

Best for: Teams that want easy adoption and to capture their know-how

Kenner gives every technician their own personal assistant, to text or call like a colleague who has been on every job.

Field service is a data-starved industry. There are metrics for everything except the most important part: the technician's work on site. Nobody has recorded the 20 diagnosis steps they tried before finding the fault.

Same as the others

  • Service reports from a conversation. Many tools can now turn a voice note into a clean service report. Kenner does the same.
  • Troubleshooting from manuals and guides. Like many others on this list.

Different to the others

  • Stores field knowledge. We use a knowledge graph to organise each new fix or method learnt and apply it to the right part, model and conditions. Neuron7 also describes a graph, built from past cases.
  • Connects across your systems. The ERP for parts, the CRM for customer notes, your knowledge base and your FSM. Kenner files notes and takes actions across all of them.
  • Captures more in every report. Kenner is curious, so it asks follow-up questions as you complete the report. Reports then always contain the root cause, the diagnosis steps and the actions. Better data means better AI.
  • Turns your field know-how into a company asset. The models are becoming the same for everyone. Your data is the competitive advantage.
  • Does the work for you. Most software is hard to use, so let AI use it for you. Kenner compares distributors and orders the part, files the service report, and prepares quotes and invoices.
  • No new app. We have had more than 200 conversations with service leaders. Two told us getting technicians to use new apps had not been a struggle. You do not need a training day to explain texting.
  • Calling. Hands-free help on an ordinary phone call. In this guide only Aquant offers the same.
  • Live in a week, free to pilot. Kenner connects to your systems in the first week, then runs a two-week pilot with 5 to 10 technicians, at no cost while it is in beta. Your technicians just need to save a contact in their phone.

Useful across industries: HVAC/R, elevators, fire protection, medical device and industrial equipment servicing.

  • The pros and cons of a young startup: security certificates are in progress and there are fewer case studies, but you work directly with the founders, get to shape the product, and get custom integrations to your software.

Pick it if you want AI that takes action, not just gives advice, field know-how that builds into a company asset, and technicians who actually use it.

Look elsewhere if your technicians already live in your suite's app and its own AI covers what you need, or you want scheduling and dispatch optimisation, a tool you can sign up for and try today, or a vendor with finished security certifications.

2 Aquant

Best for: Service organisations of equipment manufacturers

Aquant is an AI platform for equipment service that guides technicians through troubleshooting by voice, text and phone camera, and gives service leaders analytics on teams, assets and parts. It is the closest alternative to Kenner in this guide: a specialist that answers from your own service data and reaches the technician by voice call or SMS, with no app.

Type
Specialist troubleshooting platform
Answers from
Your manuals, service records and service history, plus expert knowledge Aquant captures
How technicians use it
A voice call, SMS, web or an embedded view, with the phone camera for visual guidance. No app required
What it does for the technician
Documents the repair and updates the service record
Published price
Not published. A free trial sign-up is offered

What it does well

  • Voice and phone-camera guidance on the technician's own smartphone, with no headset or smart glasses.
  • Answers are built from the customer's own manuals, service records and expert knowledge.
  • Documents the repair automatically and updates the service record.
  • Scorecards for technicians and teams, parts usage trends and cost leakage for service leaders.
  • Describes an offline mode for its AI: key data and workflows are preloaded to the device and sync when the connection returns.

Where it stops

  • Positioned for companies that manufacture physical products and service them, more than for independent contractors.
  • Aquant's pages describe troubleshooting, knowledge capture and analytics, not scheduling or dispatch.
  • Prices are not published.

Pick it if you make the equipment you service, you want voice and camera guidance without an app, and you want analytics on technicians, parts and cost alongside it.

Look elsewhere if you are an independent service contractor, or you need to see a price before you talk to sales.

Aquant or Kenner?

Choose Aquant if you manufacture equipment and service it, and you want troubleshooting guidance by voice, text and phone camera plus analytics across teams, assets and parts.

Choose Kenner if you are an independent service contractor, or you want the follow-up done as well as the diagnosis: the part ordered and the quote or invoice prepared in your ERP and CRM. Both answer an ordinary phone call.

Neuron7 or Kenner?

Questions and answers

What is the best AI for field service?

There is no single best one. If your technicians already work in your suite's app all day, its own AI is the easy first step. If getting technicians to use new software has been the hard part, or you want tasks done and not only answered, a personal assistant they text or call, such as Kenner, goes further.

What does AI actually do in field service?

Most of it informs: it briefs the technician before the job, helps troubleshoot the fault and drafts the summary afterwards, and in the office it helps plan, schedule and dispatch. A newer kind also acts: it orders the part, writes the report and prepares the quote or the invoice in the systems of record.

What is the difference between a chatbot and a personal assistant in field service?

A chatbot or copilot sits inside an app and answers when it is asked. A personal assistant is reached like a colleague, by text or phone call, and carries the task through: it finds the answer, orders the part, writes the report and prepares the quote. The first kind is what most field service suites ship today. Kenner is the second kind.

Is the AI built into my field service software enough?

For briefs and summaries, often yes. For troubleshooting it depends on where your knowledge is. Suite AI answers from what is in the suite. If the fix is in a PDF manual, in a ten-year-old work order in another system or in someone's head, check whether the tool can read it.

Why does our own service data matter for AI?

The models are available to everyone. Your service history is not. The best AI comes from the best data, so a knowledge base built from your own jobs is an asset that sets you apart from competitors, and it compounds: the sooner you start, the more it knows. Kenner builds that knowledge base for your company as technicians use it.

How much does AI for field service cost?

Few vendors say. On the pages we checked, Salesforce lists Agentforce for Field Service at $125 per user per month on top of its Field Service licences, Microsoft includes Copilot in the $105 per user per month Dynamics 365 Field Service licence, and MaintainX has plans from free to $65 per user per month. Kenner is free while in beta and has not published prices for afterwards. The rest quote on request.

Does field service AI work offline?

With no signal at all, do not count on it: the models behind these tools run on servers, not on the phone. With a phone signal but no mobile data, tools that work by phone call or SMS still answer. In this guide that is Kenner and Aquant.

Will AI replace field service technicians?

No. Every tool here works by giving a technician information or taking the paperwork and the ordering off them. The repair, the safety decisions and the responsibility stay with the person on site.

How should we evaluate an AI tool for field service?

On your own jobs. Pick one team, connect the tool to your real work orders, and measure something you already track, such as first-time fix, callbacks or time spent on reports, before and after. Then count how many technicians are still using it after two weeks. A demo on the vendor's data tells you little.
