Best AI for Field Service

Best AI for field service, by the platform you run

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

The system your work orders live in narrows the choice more than anything else. Each major suite has its own AI, which only works inside that suite. The specialists connect to several: Kenner reads from and writes back to the FSM, ERP and CRM you already run, and Neuron7 runs inside Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft and SAP.

  • Salesforce Agentforce for Field Service: the suite AI for Salesforce Field Service.
  • PTC ServiceMax AI: the AI in ServiceMax, which is built on the Salesforce platform.
  • Microsoft Copilot in Dynamics 365 Field Service: the suite AI for Dynamics 365 Field Service.
  • ServiceNow Field Service Management: the suite AI for ServiceNow.
  • SAP Field Service and Asset Management with Joule: the suite AI for SAP, with Joule.
  • IFS Cloud Field Service Management with IFS.ai: the suite AI for IFS Cloud.
  • Oracle Fusion Field Service: the suite AI for Oracle Fusion Field Service.
  • Kenner: connects to Salesforce Field Service, Dynamics 365 Field Service, ServiceNow, SAP, IFS, Oracle Field Service, ServiceMax and ServiceTitan. Our product.
  • Neuron7: runs inside Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft and SAP.

If you run one suite for everything

Switch on the suite's AI first. It needs no integration and it already sees your records. Check what it costs: Salesforce charges $125 per user per month for Agentforce for Field Service, IFS needs its IFS.ai Activation Pass or token add-on, SAP treats most generative features as premium, and Microsoft includes Copilot in the licence.

If your data is spread across systems

Many service organisations have work orders in one system, parts in an ERP, manuals on a shared drive and alarms in something else. Suite AI answers from the suite. A specialist that connects to all of them can answer from all of them, which is the reason to add one.

If you might change systems

Suite AI stays with the suite. If a migration is on the horizon, the knowledge you build up in a specialist that sits on top is the part that moves with you.

Questions and answers

What is the best AI for Salesforce Field Service?

Agentforce for Field Service is Salesforce's own and the most complete of the suite assistants we compared. Neuron7 and Kenner both work with Salesforce if you need answers from history and manuals beyond what is in it.

What is the best AI for Dynamics 365 Field Service?

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Field Service, because it is included in the licence. Its documented features are summaries and record updates, so for troubleshooting guidance you would add a specialist.

Does Kenner replace the field service system?

No. It reads from the FSM, ERP and CRM before the job and writes the report, parts and follow-ups back after it.
